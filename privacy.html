KayaKnow (“the app”) is provided by Iron Future, Hong Kong (“we”). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no account or sign-up and does not ask for your name, phone number or email address.

2. Data stored on your device

Everything you enter (income, spending, budgets, bills, debts, savings goals, settings and your PIN) is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

3. Analytics, ads and tracking

The app has no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking tools.

4. HK Money Notes

When your phone is online, the app may download a public file of money notes from this website. Nothing about you or your records is sent. Like any website, the hosting service may briefly log technical information such as an IP address for security; we do not use it to identify you.

5. Backups

If you create a backup, the app saves a file to a place you choose, or opens your phone’s share menu. Where that file goes is your choice. We never receive it.

6. Notifications

If you add a bill, the app may ask permission to send reminders. Reminders are scheduled on your device, and you can turn them off in your phone’s settings at any time.

7. Links to other apps

If you tap a link (for example, to contact us on WhatsApp or open a web page), you leave the app, and that service’s own privacy policy applies.

8. Purchases

If the app offers paid features in the future, payment will be handled by Google Play. We will not receive your payment card details.

9. Deleting your data

You can delete all data with “Erase and start again” in the app’s settings, or by uninstalling the app. Because we hold no data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

10. Children

The app is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

11. Not financial advice

KayaKnow provides general information and calculation tools only. We do not lend money and do not work with any lender.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be on this page.
